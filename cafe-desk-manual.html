Part 2

Where to Start — 5-Minute Installation & First-Time Shop Setup

Follow these 4 steps in exact order whenever you install Café Desk on a new client computer via USB:

1

Copy from USB to the Shop Computer

Plug in your USB stick and either:

  • Run CafeDesk-Setup.exe to install Desktop & Start Menu shortcuts automatically, OR
  • Copy the entire CafeDesk folder (including _internal) onto the PC (for example C:\CafeDesk) and send a shortcut of CafeDesk.exe to the Desktop. (Copying onto the PC’s drive makes startup much faster than running off a USB stick.)
2

Activate the 1-Year Computer License

  • Open CafeDesk.exe. The Activate Café Desk window opens showing that computer’s unique hardware Machine ID (for example: CD-8F3A-91BC-204E).
  • On your own private laptop, generate their 365-day activation key: 
    python admin_keygen.py -m CD-8F3A-91BC-204E -c "Sunrise Cyber Cafe" -d 365
  • Paste the CDLIC1... key (or load the .cafelicense file) into Step 2 on the customer’s screen and click ✓ Activate This Computer.
3

Create the Shop’s Staff PIN & Branding

  • Click ⚙ Business settings on the left sidebar.
  • Because no PIN is set yet, Café Desk prompts the shop owner to choose a 6–12 digit Staff PIN. Have the owner type and confirm their secret PIN.
  • Type their Business Name (up to 48 characters), pick one of the 10 icons or click Upload logo to add their shop logo, and click Save business settings.
4

Enter the Shop’s Service Price List (& Optional OCR)

  • Click ≡ Price settings, enter their currency symbol (e.g. R), and type one service per line using Service Name | Price: 
    Black & White Printing per page | 3.00
Colour Printing per page | 8.00
Scanning to PDF per page | 5.00
Full CV Typing | 50.00
Cover Letter | 25.00
Affidavit / Form Typing | 20.00
A4 Photo Sheet | 25.00
  • Click Save prices. Everyone can now search prices in Price list and build receipts in Receipts!
  • Optional for OCR: Open ? Help & privacy and click Download Tesseract OCR (Windows .exe) (keep English language data ticked during setup).

Important Note on Sales Reports & Kiosk Accounts

Sales reports are a convenient local counter log (not a fiscal VAT/tax POS register). On shared walk-in computers, always run Windows under a standard (non-Administrator) user account and click Next customer after every client.

Part 6

Owner Cheat Sheet — Licensing, Renewals & PC Performance FAQ

What if a buyer copies CafeDesk to another PC?

It will immediately stop at the Activate Café Desk screen! Every license key is cryptographically signed (Ed25519) for one specific computer’s hardware Machine ID and stored per-PC in %APPDATA%\CafeDesk\license.json.

What happens after 1 year (365 days)?

After 365 days, Café Desk locks cleanly and asks the shop owner to send you their Machine ID to purchase a 1-year renewal key. Built-in clock-rollback detection blocks users from rewinding their Windows date.

Will it overheat old PCs or eat RAM?

No — idle CPU is ~0% and typical RAM is only 65–130 MB (far lighter than browser/Electron apps). Built-in safety caps block oversized images (>50 MP / 60 MB) and giant PDFs (>2,000 pages).

How do I generate keys as the Owner?

Run python admin_keygen.py --init-keys --apply-public-key once on your own laptop before building your customer .exe. To approve the paid PDF Password Remover add-on for a customer’s Machine ID, run python admin_keygen.py --addon pdf_unlock -m CD-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX -c "Shop Name" (or choose option [2] in interactive mode). Never copy admin_keygen.py or owner-keys/ onto customer USB sticks!
