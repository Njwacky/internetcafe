How it works

From this page to a printed document in three steps

Templates are used inside the Café Desk app on the counter PC — this page is the window onto the same catalogue.

Step 01

Download Café Desk

Install the free Windows app on the counter PC and activate it with your shop key.

Go to downloads
Step 02

Open Online Templates

In the app, open Online Templates and pick a category — CV, Card, Form or Collage. The same list you see above loads there.

Step 03

Download & print

Tap Download on a template. It is saved on the PC and ready to use in the matching workspace — no internet needed afterwards.

Shop owner? To add a template, open the owner dashboard → Online templates, fill in the slug, name and JSON definition, and publish. It appears on this page and in every Café Desk PC automatically. Only active templates are public — hidden ones stay private.
